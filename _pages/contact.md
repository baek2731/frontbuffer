Frontbuffer is published by Frontbuffer Editorial. The best way to reach us is by email.

**Email:** frontbuffer.editorial@gmail.com

**X:** [@FrontbufferEdit](https://x.com/FrontbufferEdit)

## Corrections

If you find an error in an article, write to us with the article's URL, the sentence that looks wrong, and a source that shows what is correct. An official support page, a manufacturer specification or a primary announcement is the most useful kind of source. When we confirm a correction, we update the article and its "Checked" date.

## What to include

- **For a correction:** the article's URL, the sentence in question and a source.
- **For a problem with a guide:** your device or software model and version, and the step where the guide differs from what you see. Menu names change between versions, so this helps us check the right page.
- **For a source or topic suggestion:** a link and a sentence on why it matters.

We do not promise a response time.

## Source and topic suggestions

If an official page we cited has moved, or you know of a primary source we missed, send us the link. You can also tell us about a change in a product, setting or policy that you think readers are searching for.
