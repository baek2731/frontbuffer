### How our articles are made

Frontbuffer articles are researched from official documentation, manufacturer support pages and announcements first, and from press reports when an official page does not exist or when the news itself is the subject. Each article ends with the list of sources it relies on. Most articles also say when they were last checked, because menus, prices and policies change.

We do not test devices or apps ourselves unless an article says so. When the steps or figures in an article come from a manufacturer page, a developer document or a report, the article says which one. If sources disagree, or a point rests on a single user's report or a single secondary source, we say that in the text. We leave out claims that we cannot trace to a source.

### Use of AI tools

We use AI-assisted tools to help research and draft articles. The tools are not treated as a source. Factual claims are compared against the sources listed at the end of each article, and statements that cannot be matched to a source are removed. AI tools can still make mistakes, so please tell us if you find one.

### Corrections

If you spot an error, write to us through our [Contact](/contact/) page with the article's URL, the sentence in question and a source for the correct information. When we confirm a correction, we update the article and its checked date.

### Affiliate disclosure

Some articles on this site may contain affiliate links. If you purchase a product through one of these links, we may earn a small commission at no additional cost to you. Affiliate relationships do not influence our editorial coverage or recommendations.

### Advertising

This site does not currently display advertisements. If we start showing ads, they will be served by third parties such as Google AdSense, and ad placement will not influence editorial content. Our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy/) explains how advertising and analytics cookies are handled.
