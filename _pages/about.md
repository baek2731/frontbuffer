**Frontbuffer** is an independent tech and gaming site. It publishes guides, fixes and comparisons for the questions people search for when a feature changes, a setting moves or something stops working.

## What we cover

**Tech:** Android, Pixel and Galaxy features, Samsung Health and Health Connect, Google services such as Messages and Android Auto, and Chrome extensions affected by the move to Manifest V3.

**Gaming:** Steam Machine, Steam Deck and Steam Frame, portable PC handhelds, PC graphics hardware and the platforms and stores games run on.

We pick topics where a reader has to make a decision or fix a problem. A typical article explains what changed, lists the steps or the differences, and says what we could not confirm.

## How we research and write

- **Official sources first.** We start with manufacturer support pages, developer documentation and announcements. We add press reports when an official page does not exist or when the news itself is the subject. Every article ends with a list of its sources.
- **Dated checks.** Menus, prices and policies change, so most articles say when they were last checked. When something changes, we update the article and its date.
- **No hands-on testing unless stated.** We do not claim to have tested a device or an app unless the article says so. Where the steps come from a manufacturer page or a report, the article says that.
- **Conflicts and thin evidence are labeled.** If two sources disagree, we show both. If a claim rests on one user's report or a single secondary source, we say so, and we leave out claims that we cannot trace to any source.
- **AI assistance.** We use AI tools to help research and draft articles. Each claim is compared against the sources listed in the article. Our [Disclosure](/disclosure/) page explains this in more detail.

## What we do not do

We do not present rumors or leaks as facts. When an article discusses one, it is labeled as a report and attributed to its source. We also do not copy text from other sites. We summarize in our own words, keep any quotation short and link to the original.

## Corrections

If you find an error, write to us through the [Contact](/contact/) page with the article's URL, the sentence that looks wrong and a source for the correct information. We update the article and its checked date once we confirm the correction.
