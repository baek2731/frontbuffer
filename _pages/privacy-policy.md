## Overview

Frontbuffer ("we", "us", or "our") operates frontbuffer.net. This page explains what information is collected when you visit the site, how it is used, and what choices you have.

## Information We Collect

We do not ask visitors for personal information, and the site has no accounts or comment forms. If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message, and we use them only to reply. Third-party services we use may collect data about your visit, as described below. Our hosting and content delivery providers may also keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses and requested pages, to deliver the site and keep it secure.

## Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors use our site. Google Analytics may collect information such as the pages you visit and how long you stay, your approximate location, your device and browser type, and the website that referred you. It uses cookies or similar identifiers for this purpose. Google processes this data under its own policies. You can opt out with the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## Advertising

This site does not currently display advertisements. If we start using Google AdSense, third-party vendors, including Google, will use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and other sites. You can manage personalized advertising in [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads), and you can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). We will update this policy before ads are shown.

## Cookies

Our site uses cookies through Google Analytics and, if we enable advertising, through Google AdSense. You can control or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect how some third-party features work.

## Third-Party Links

Articles on Frontbuffer contain links to external sites, mainly official documentation and news sources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

## Children's Privacy

Frontbuffer does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## GDPR: European Users

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including the right to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. The third-party services we use, such as Google Analytics, process data in accordance with their own compliance policies. For data requests, contact us through our [Contact](/contact/) page.

## CCPA: California Users

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information is collected about you and the right to request its deletion. We do not sell personal information. For CCPA requests, contact us through our [Contact](/contact/) page.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

## Contact

For privacy-related questions, write to frontbuffer.editorial@gmail.com or use our [Contact](/contact/) page.
