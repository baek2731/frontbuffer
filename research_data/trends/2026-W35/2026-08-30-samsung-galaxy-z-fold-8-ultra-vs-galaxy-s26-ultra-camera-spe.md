The Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra and Galaxy S26 Ultra are Samsung's two most capable cameras in 2026, released four months apart. The S26 Ultra launched March 11, the Fold 8 Ultra on August 7. Both run Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5. Their camera systems take different approaches.

## Camera Hardware: Side by Side

| | Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra | Galaxy S26 Ultra |
|---|---|---|
| Main sensor | 200MP, f/1.7 | 200MP, f/1.4 |
| Ultrawide | 50MP, f/1.9 | 50MP, f/1.9 |
| Telephoto 1 | 50MP, 3x optical | 50MP, 3x optical |
| Telephoto 2 | 50MP, 5x optical | 50MP, 5x optical (periscope) |
| Front (cover) | 10MP | 12MP |
| Front (inner) | 4MP under-display | — |
| Video | 8K@30fps | 8K@30fps |

Both devices share Samsung's 200MP main sensor platform. The S26 Ultra's f/1.4 aperture is wider than the Fold 8 Ultra's f/1.7 — a meaningful difference in low-light conditions.

## Main Camera: Where S26 Ultra Has the Edge

The S26 Ultra's f/1.4 aperture lets in more light per frame than the Fold 8 Ultra's f/1.7. In practical terms, the S26 Ultra's main camera produces cleaner shots in dim conditions without night mode. The Fold 8 Ultra compensates with software processing, but the hardware gap is real.

The S26 Ultra also benefits from better thermal management in its slab form factor — sustained 8K video capture is more reliable than on the Fold 8 Ultra, where the thinner chassis limits heat dissipation.

## Telephoto: Where Fold 8 Ultra Has the Edge

Both devices have 3x and 5x optical zoom, but the Fold 8 Ultra's dual-telephoto system covers the mid-range (3x) with a dedicated lens rather than interpolating. Portrait shots at 3x on the Fold 8 Ultra are cleaner than digital zoom from a single periscope lens covering that range.

## Flex Mode and Cover Screen Selfies

The Fold 8 Ultra's foldable design adds two camera capabilities the S26 Ultra cannot match:

**Flex Mode shooting.** Folded at 90 degrees, the Fold 8 Ultra stands on its own as a mini tripod. The lower half of the inner display acts as a shutter control and preview. Hands-free stable shots without a separate tripod.

**Cover screen selfies with the main camera.** Pointing the rear cameras at yourself with the cover screen as viewfinder gives selfie quality equivalent to the main 200MP system. The S26 Ultra's 12MP front camera is good; it's not 200MP.

## Which to Choose

**Choose the S26 Ultra** if main camera low-light performance and sustained video recording are the priority. The f/1.4 aperture and better thermal headroom are real advantages.

**Choose the Fold 8 Ultra** if you want the dual-telephoto system, Flex Mode, or cover screen selfies with the main camera. The camera trade-off versus the S26 Ultra is narrow; the form factor difference is not.
