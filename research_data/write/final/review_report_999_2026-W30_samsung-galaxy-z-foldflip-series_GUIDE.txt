# How to Optimize Cover Screen Apps on Galaxy Z Flip 8

The Samsung Galaxy Z Flip series continues to redefine the compact foldable smartphone experience. With the Galaxy Z Flip 8 officially announced on July 22, 2026, at Samsung's Galaxy Unpacked event in London, England, and set for release on August 7, 2026, user interest in maximizing its versatile cover screen, known as the Flex Window, is at an all-time high. This external display offers quick access to essential information and functions without unfolding the device, and expanding its utility by enabling a wider range of applications is a significant area of interest for users. This guide explores the foundational methods for expanding cover screen app functionality on the Galaxy Z Flip 8, building upon insights from previous models in the Galaxy Z Flip series and incorporating the latest enhancements.

### Evolution of Cover Screen App Functionality on Galaxy Z Flip Series

Earlier iterations of the Galaxy Z Flip, such as the Galaxy Z Flip 5, Galaxy Z Flip 6, and Galaxy Z Flip 7, offered a degree of cover screen customization, primarily through widgets and a limited selection of officially supported apps via the "Labs" feature in settings. For instance, the Galaxy Z Flip 5's Flex Window allowed users to access widgets for weather, calendar, and direct dial contacts, and run a few select apps like Messages, YouTube, and Netflix through its "Apps allowed on cover screen" Labs feature. Similarly, the Galaxy Z Flip 6 expanded on this, offering more widgets and a 4x4 grid layout for cover screen pages.

However, to truly unlock the potential of running virtually any app on the cover screen, users of these earlier models often relied on Samsung's Good Lock suite, specifically the MultiStar module. This unofficial, yet Samsung-developed, customization tool became indispensable for power users seeking to bypass the default limitations and transform their cover screen into a more powerful mini-interface. The Galaxy Z Flip 7, released on July 25, 2025, also benefited significantly from MultiStar, enabling users to run apps like Google Maps and Gmail directly from its larger 4.1-inch cover screen.

### Galaxy Z Flip 8's Enhanced Native Support and Streamlined Optimization

The Galaxy Z Flip 8 marks a significant step forward in native cover screen app support. Samsung has expanded the Flex Window's capabilities, allowing 16 apps to run directly on the outer display without requiring third-party workarounds. This includes 12 fully optimized system apps such as Bixby, Samsung Browser, Calculator, Calendar, Camera, Clock, Contacts, Gallery, Samsung Health, Notes, Phone, and Voice Recorder, all accessible through a native app drawer. Additionally, four key third-party applications—Google Maps, Google Messages, Netflix, and YouTube—are now grouped under a "Labs" section, offering a more integrated experience, though Samsung notes that performance may vary.

Crucially, for apps not natively optimized, the Galaxy Z Flip 8 simplifies the process of leveraging MultiStar. Instead of requiring users to navigate through complex settings while the phone is unfolded, the Z Flip 8 now provides a prompt directly on the cover screen to guide users through the installation and setup of the Good Lock MultiStar module. This enhancement addresses a long-standing user complaint and brings Samsung's cover screen flexibility closer to that of rivals like Motorola's Razr series.

### Expanding Cover Screen Functionality with MultiStar

For applications beyond the Z Flip 8's new native support, MultiStar remains the go-to solution for comprehensive cover screen customization. MultiStar is a module within Samsung's Good Lock app, a powerful suite of customization tools available exclusively for Samsung Galaxy users via the Galaxy Store.

To enable a wider range of applications on your Galaxy Z Flip 8's cover screen using MultiStar, follow these steps:

1.  **Download Good Lock:** Open the Galaxy Store on your Z Flip 8, search for "Good Lock," and install the application.
2.  **Install MultiStar Module:** Launch the Good Lock app. Navigate to the "Life Up" tab (or "Plugins" on older versions) at the bottom, locate the "MultiStar" module, and tap to install it.
3.  **Access "I ♡ Galaxy Foldable" Settings:** Open the MultiStar module. You will typically find an option titled "I ♡ Galaxy Foldable" or a direct path to "Apps allowed on cover screen" within Labs that integrates with MultiStar.
4.  **Enable Launcher Widget:** Within the foldable-specific settings, select "Launcher Widget." This feature allows you to create a customizable widget that can house any app installed on your device.
5.  **Select Desired Apps:** From the list of applications, select all the apps you wish to access from your cover screen.
6.  **Add the Launcher Widget to Cover Screen:** Go to your phone's main "Settings," then navigate to "Cover screen" (or "Flex Window") and tap "Widgets." Scroll down to find and add the "Good Lock Launcher" widget to your cover screen.

Once the widget is added, you can fold your phone, activate the cover screen, and swipe left until you locate the new Launcher widget. Tapping on it will reveal your selected apps, ready for quick access without unfolding the device. While some apps may not be perfectly optimized for the smaller display, MultiStar significantly enhances the cover screen's utility for quick tasks and information access.

### Beyond Basic Apps: Advanced MultiStar Features

MultiStar's capabilities extend beyond simply launching apps on the cover screen. It also offers several features that enhance the overall multitasking experience on Samsung Galaxy devices, including foldable phones:

*   **Multi Focus:** This feature allows apps in split-screen or pop-up view to run continuously without pausing, even when they are not the active window. This is particularly useful for watching videos while typing or keeping a navigation app active alongside another application.
*   **Multi-window Screen Zoom:** MultiStar can reduce the screen size of apps in multi-window mode, allowing more content to be displayed, which is especially beneficial on larger foldable displays.
*   **Flexible Window Resizing:** It offers more granular control over window resizing in split-screen, moving beyond Samsung's default preset ratios.
*   **Samsung DeX Enhancements:** For users who utilize Samsung DeX, MultiStar provides upgrades like higher-resolution output for external displays, the ability to run more simultaneous apps in DeX mode, and automatic re-launch of recently used apps.

These advanced features underscore MultiStar's role as a comprehensive tool for power users seeking to maximize their Galaxy Z Flip 8's multitasking and external display capabilities.
