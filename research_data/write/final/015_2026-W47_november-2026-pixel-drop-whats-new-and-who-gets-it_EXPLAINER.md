# November 2026 Pixel Drop: What's New and Who Gets It

Google's November 2026 Pixel Drop brings Gemini Intelligence to the Pixel 10, including Rambler voice typing, and adds a Call Screen upgrade for the Pixel 11 series. Smaller items such as custom icon styles and Pixel search reach older phones. Google says the updates started rolling out on November 10 and will continue over the next several weeks, and most features depend on your model, country or language.

*Checked November 18, 2026 against Google's November Pixel Drop post and its Android blog post on Gemini Intelligence, both published November 10, 2026. We did not test any of these features.*

## What is in it

| Feature | What Google says it does | Who gets it |
|---|---|---|
| Rambler | Voice typing that removes filler words such as "um" and formats speech into text you review and send | Pixel 10 models, in Gboard, in select countries and languages, with usage limits |
| Multi-step tasks with Gemini | Handles errands such as groceries, ride booking, takeout and reservations | Pixel 10 (except Pixel 10a) or newer, with the Gemini app, users 18 and older, in select countries and languages. Google's Drop post lists Japan, the UK and India |
| Proactive suggestions | Offers timely actions, such as sharing your ETA | Same requirements as multi-step tasks |
| Custom widgets | Builds a widget from a text prompt, such as a spending tracker or a trip countdown | Pixel 10, 10 Pro, 10 Pro XL, 10 Pro Fold, 11, 11 Pro, 11 Pro XL and 11 Pro Fold, in select markets |
| Device Assistance | Lets you ask Gemini to change or fix device settings, such as clearing storage or checking app location access | "Select Android devices running Android 17 or later", with no device list |
| Call Screen upgrade | Gemini asks unknown callers why they are calling and shows a live transcript | Pixel 11, 11 Pro, 11 Pro XL and 11 Pro Fold, US only |
| Custom icon styles | More styles that apply across all home screen app icons | Pixel 6a and newer, all regions and languages |
| Pixel search | Searches photos, emails, messages, calendar events and device settings | Pixel 6 and newer, per the post's text |
| HiLight for Google Messages | The phone glows when it is face down and a favorite contact messages you | Pixel 11 Pro, 11 Pro XL and 11 Pro Fold |

Google's post also covers Pixel Watch features, a $29.99 Pixel Tag tracker that Google says is available from November 11, and a Google Fi network update for eligible Pixels, Pixel 9 or newer. We list only phone features above.

## Our reading by phone

This is our reading of Google's footnotes, not a Google statement. Check the footnote for your exact model.

- **Pixel 11 series:** the widest set, including Call Screen, custom widgets and, on the Pro models, HiLight.
- **Pixel 10, 10 Pro, 10 Pro XL, 10 Pro Fold:** Rambler, multi-step tasks, proactive suggestions and custom widgets.
- **Pixel 10a:** Google excludes it from proactive suggestions and multi-step tasks, and it is not in the custom widgets list. Its Rambler support is not stated, because the Pixel 10a is not named in the Rambler text. Icon styles and Pixel search are listed for Pixel 6a and newer.
- **Pixel 9 and older:** of the phone features in the table, only custom icon styles (Pixel 6a and newer) and Pixel search (Pixel 6 or 6a and newer) list them.

## Where Google's own pages differ

- **Pixel search.** The post's text says Pixel 6 and newer, while its footnote says Pixel 6a and newer.
- **Multi-step tasks.** The Drop post says they are "now available" in Japan, the UK and India. The Android blog post says they are expanding to those countries.
- **Pixel 10 models.** In the Android blog post, one sentence says the Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro XL and Pixel 10 Pro Fold get the latest Gemini Intelligence features, while the Rambler sentence also lists the Pixel 10 Pro. We read the Pixel 10 Pro as included, but the post does not say so in one place.

## How to get it

Pixel Drops arrive as software updates. Google's post does not give menu steps. Open Settings, then System, then Software updates, and check for an update. Menu names may differ by Android version, and we did not check this path on a Pixel. Because Google says the rollout runs over several weeks, a feature may not show up on the day you update.

## What we could not confirm

- When each feature reaches your phone, since Google gives a rollout window and not dates per device.
- The phone requirement for conversation summaries, which Google's footnote does not state clearly.
- Which devices count as "select Android devices" for Device Assistance.
- Whether the Pixel 10a gets Rambler, since neither post names it.
- How Rambler's usage limits work, since the Android blog post mentions limits without details.
- How any of these features perform, because we did not test them.
