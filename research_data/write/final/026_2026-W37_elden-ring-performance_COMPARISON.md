# Elden Ring PC vs Console performance differences explained

FromSoftware’s *Elden Ring* captivated players with its sprawling open world and challenging combat, quickly becoming a landmark title in the action RPG genre. However, the experience of traversing the Lands Between has varied significantly depending on the platform, particularly regarding performance on PC versus consoles. Upon its February 2022 release, PC players reported widespread stuttering and crashes, issues largely absent from the more consistent console versions. This article details those initial disparities, the community's swift interventions, and FromSoftware's ongoing optimization efforts.

## The Rocky Launch of Elden Ring on PC

Upon its release in February 2022, *Elden Ring* on PC was met with significant performance concerns, overshadowing some of its critical acclaim. Many users and critics alike noted that the game's PC port suffered from optimization issues. Digital Foundry, a respected technical analysis outlet, highlighted pervasive stuttering, particularly when traversing the open world or during new asset loading, attributing some of these issues to the game's use of DirectX 12 (DX12) and inefficient asset streaming. This led to a widespread perception among the community that the game's performance was subpar for a AAA title.

Initial reviews of the game often focused on gameplay and artistic merit, with detailed performance analyses sometimes following later, which many players found frustrating given the widespread issues. Digital Foundry's early technical breakdown underscored the PC version's struggles, noting that even on high-end hardware, consistent frame rates were elusive, and the experience felt "compromised" compared to what was expected from a modern PC title.

## Early PC Performance: More Than Just Stutters

The performance issues on PC extended beyond mere frame rate drops and stuttering. Numerous players reported severe problems that significantly hampered their ability to play the game. These included frequent game crashes, instances where the game would close itself unexpectedly, and even system reboots during gameplay. Connection drops were also a persistent annoyance for many PC players, particularly impacting the online multiplayer experience.

The cumulative effect of these problems led to considerable frustration within the PC gaming community. Many players expressed disappointment with the state of PC game launches, where day-one issues have become increasingly common, leading to a sentiment of caution regarding future FromSoftware PC ports.

## Official Responses and Community Interventions

In response to the widespread performance complaints, FromSoftware and Bandai Namco acknowledged the issues shortly after launch. They released several patches aimed at addressing frame rate stability, stuttering, and other bugs. For instance, Patch 1.02.2, released in early March 2022, specifically targeted frame rate stability and other performance-related issues on PC. Subsequent patches continued to refine performance and address various technical glitches.

In the absence of immediate comprehensive official fixes, the *Elden Ring* community quickly mobilized to address some of the PC version's shortcomings. Within hours of the game's release, community members developed and released unofficial tools such as an FPS unlocker and an ultrawide mod. These fan-made solutions allowed some players with powerful hardware, like an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2080 Super, to achieve significantly higher frame rates, often reaching 80-100fps+ in less demanding areas, by bypassing the game's 60fps cap. While such community efforts provided temporary relief, they also underscored the initial state of the official port and the demand for better optimization.

## The Console Experience: A Different Perspective

While the PC version faced significant hurdles, the console experience, particularly on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X, generally offered a more consistent, albeit not flawless, performance profile at launch. Digital Foundry's analysis indicated that both current-gen consoles offered a relatively stable 60 frames per second (fps) in "Performance Mode," though with dynamic resolution scaling. The PlayStation 5 version typically maintained a higher average resolution in this mode compared to the Xbox Series X. In "Quality Mode," both consoles targeted 30fps at higher resolutions, but often struggled to maintain a locked frame rate, experiencing drops in demanding areas.

The key distinction was the absence of the severe stuttering and widespread crashing issues that plagued the PC version. While console players also encountered occasional frame drops, the overall stability and "plug-and-play" nature of the experience were generally superior to the initial PC launch. This disparity in reported issues strongly indicated that the PC port bore the brunt of optimization problems, making the console versions the more reliable option for a smoother day-one experience.

## Lingering Concerns and the Shadow of the Erdtree DLC

Despite numerous official patches since its 2022 launch, some performance concerns for *Elden Ring* on PC persisted for a segment of the player base. While the most egregious day-one issues were largely mitigated, occasional stuttering and inconsistent frame delivery remained points of contention for some players even into 2024.

With the highly anticipated "Shadow of the Erdtree" DLC released in June 2024, performance once again became a focal point. While the DLC itself was critically acclaimed, some PC players reported new or exacerbated performance issues upon its release, including frame rate drops in new areas and occasional stuttering. However, FromSoftware quickly released further patches to address these specific DLC-related performance hiccups, aiming to stabilize the experience. The ongoing need for post-launch and post-DLC performance adjustments highlights the continuous challenge of optimizing complex open-world titles across diverse PC hardware configurations.

## Navigating the Lands Between: Recommendations for PC Players

For PC players venturing into *Elden Ring*, understanding the game's performance evolution since its February 2022 launch is key. While official patches, including those for the 'Shadow of the Erdtree' DLC, have addressed many initial issues, occasional stuttering and inconsistent frame delivery can persist. To optimize your journey through the Lands Between, consult reputable [PC optimization guides for Elden Ring](https://www.pcgamer.com/elden-ring-pc-performance-guide/) for settings adjustments and consider community-developed solutions like FPS unlockers or ultrawide mods, which have historically offered significant improvements for powerful hardware like an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2080 Super. Ultimately, achieving a consistently smooth experience in *Elden Ring* on PC demands more than just powerful hardware; it requires diligent application of specific patches and a readiness to explore community fixes to overcome the game's unique optimization quirks, a level of engagement rarely needed for the more stable console versions. Therefore, to truly conquer the Lands Between on PC without performance interruptions, players must go beyond standard driver updates, actively seeking out specific patches like 1.02.2 and 1.12.1, and considering community tools that address *Elden Ring*'s particular stuttering and frame rate limitations.
