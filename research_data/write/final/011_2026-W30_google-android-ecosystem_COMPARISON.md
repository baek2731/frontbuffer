# Google Pixel Launcher vs Nova Launcher vs Action Launcher: Which Should You Use?

The Pixel Launcher ships on every Pixel phone and represents Google's intended Android experience. Nova Launcher has been the default choice for Android power users since 2011. Action Launcher takes a different approach, prioritizing efficiency features over raw customization depth. Choosing between them comes down to what you actually want to change about your home screen.

---

## Pixel Launcher

Google's stock launcher is built around deep integration with its own services. The At a Glance widget surfaces calendar events, weather, traffic alerts, and package delivery statuses without opening any app. The search bar connects directly to Google Search, Assistant, and Lens. App suggestions in the drawer learn from usage patterns over time.

Themed Icons, introduced with Android 12, automatically adapts app icon colors to match your wallpaper — a feature that requires no configuration and works across system and third-party apps that support it.

The Pixel Launcher's limitations are deliberate. Grid size, icon appearance, and gesture behavior aren't configurable. If you want those controls, you need a third-party launcher.

---

## Nova Launcher

Nova has maintained its user base for over a decade by offering granular control over nearly every aspect of the home screen. Custom grid layouts, icon pack support, gesture assignments, dock customization, and folder appearance are all adjustable. A backup and restore feature makes it easy to transfer configurations between devices.

In 2022, Nova Launcher was acquired by Branch Metrics. Nova Launcher 8.0 followed with significant improvements and a refreshed interface, addressing initial concerns about the acquisition's impact on development.

Nova Launcher Prime (the paid version) unlocks gesture controls and additional customization options. The free version covers most use cases.

→ [Nova Launcher on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.teslacoilsw.launcher)

---

## Action Launcher

Action Launcher focuses on efficiency rather than visual customization. Its standout features are designed to reduce the number of taps needed to reach content:

**Shutters** — swipe up on an app icon to reveal its widget inline, without adding it to the home screen. Useful for weather, calendar, or music controls.

**Covers** — tap a folder to launch its first app directly; swipe to open the full folder. Eliminates the extra tap that standard folders require.

**Quickdrawer** — an always-accessible alphabetical app list, faster to navigate than a standard app drawer for large libraries.

**Quicktheme** — automatically themes the launcher using colors extracted from your wallpaper, similar to Pixel Launcher's Themed Icons but broader in scope.

→ [Action Launcher on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.actionlauncher.playstore)

---

## Which One to Use

**Stay with Pixel Launcher if** you use a Pixel phone and don't feel friction with the default experience. The At a Glance widget and Themed Icons are genuinely useful, and the launcher is tightly optimized for Pixel hardware.

**Switch to Nova Launcher if** you want control over grid size, icon packs, or gesture behavior. It's the most battle-tested third-party option with the largest community and widest icon pack support.

**Try Action Launcher if** you find yourself frequently tapping into folders or widgets. Shutters and Covers solve real navigation friction that Nova doesn't address.
