# What's New in the Latest Pixel Drop and How to Use the New Features

Google's Pixel Feature Drops consistently introduce new functionalities and refinements to Pixel devices, enhancing the user experience with both major features and subtle quality-of-life improvements. These updates, often anticipated by the community, aim to keep the Pixel ecosystem fresh and competitive. ### Recent Enhancements and Features

The June 2024 Pixel Feature Drop brought a suite of significant updates, particularly for AI capabilities and communication. A standout feature is the expansion of **Gemini Nano** to the Pixel 8, bringing advanced on-device AI directly to more users. This enables features like Summarize in Recorder and more capable Gboard Smart Reply, enhancing productivity and communication without relying on cloud processing. To utilize Gemini Nano, users typically need to ensure their device is updated to the latest software version and then access compatible applications like Recorder or Gboard.

Another notable addition is **DisplayPort support** for the Pixel 8 Pro, allowing users to connect their device to an external monitor via a USB-C to DisplayPort cable. This transforms the Pixel 8 Pro into a more versatile workstation, ideal for presentations, media consumption, or extended productivity. Users can simply connect their device to a compatible display, and the phone's interface will adapt to the larger screen.

For communication, the June 2024 drop introduced **Reverse Phone Number Lookup** directly within the Phone app. If an unknown number calls, users can now perform a quick lookup to identify the caller, helping to screen unwanted calls and enhance personal security. This feature is integrated into the call history, making it easily accessible after receiving a call from an unfamiliar number.

The December 2023 Pixel Feature Drop also delivered several key improvements. **Video Boost** was extended to the Pixel 8 Pro, enhancing video quality by adjusting color, lighting, and stabilization after recording, leveraging Google's computational photography. This feature processes videos in the cloud, delivering a polished, professional-looking result. Users can activate Video Boost within the Camera app settings before recording.

**Night Sight for Timelapse** arrived on the Pixel 8 and Pixel 8 Pro, allowing users to capture stunning timelapses in low-light conditions. This combines the power of Night Sight with timelapse photography, opening up new creative possibilities for capturing dynamic scenes from dusk till dawn. Accessing this feature is done through the Camera app's modes.

For Pixel Fold and Pixel Tablet users, the December 2023 drop brought enhanced multitasking and productivity. **Clear Calling** was made available on the Pixel Fold, improving call quality by reducing background noise. The **Pixel Tablet** received updates to its Gboard, enabling **handwriting recognition** in more languages, making it easier to input text with a stylus. Additionally, **Photo Unblur** was extended to the Pixel Fold and Pixel Tablet, allowing users to sharpen blurry photos directly on their devices.
