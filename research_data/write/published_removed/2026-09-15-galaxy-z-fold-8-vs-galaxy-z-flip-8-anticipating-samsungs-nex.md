Samsung announced the Galaxy Z Fold 8 and Z Flip 8 at Galaxy Unpacked in London on July 22, 2026, alongside the Z Fold 8 Ultra. Both the Fold 8 and Flip 8 run Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy, Android 17 with One UI 9, and share IP48 dust and water resistance. The decision between them comes down to what you actually use a phone for.

---

---

## Display: Different Problems, Different Solutions

The Z Fold 8 uses a 7.6-inch 4:3 main display — the same aspect ratio as the iPhone Duo — designed for side-by-side apps, document editing, and video. At 4.5mm when open, it's thinner than most non-folding flagships. The 5.5-inch cover screen is large enough for most tasks without unfolding.

The Z Flip 8's 6.9-inch display is a standard tall-format phone screen. The 4.1-inch FlexWindow cover screen — up from 3.4 inches on the Flip 7 — handles notifications, quick replies, and camera access without opening the device. When folded, the Flip 8 is 86mm tall, which fits pockets and small bags where the Fold 8 doesn't.

---

## Camera: Flip 8 Has the Edge Here

The Z Fold 8's dual 12MP system is a step back from the Fold 8 Ultra's 200MP main camera. The trade-off is the thinner body — fitting a larger sensor array into the Fold 8's 4.5mm frame wasn't possible. For serious camera work, the Fold 8 Ultra is the better choice in this lineup.

The Z Flip 8 gets the 50MP FlexCam, which works in both standard and flex-angle shooting. The clamshell hinge lets the phone stand on its own for tripod-free group shots or video calls, and the large cover screen functions as a viewfinder for selfies using the main camera.

---

## Battery and Charging

The Fold 8's 4,800mAh battery charges at 45W. Given the larger display, real-world battery life is comparable to the Flip 8 rather than significantly better. The Flip 8's 4,300mAh at 25W charges more slowly — a practical consideration for heavy users.

---

## Who Should Buy Which

**Galaxy Z Fold 8** — You split-screen apps regularly, watch video on your phone, or want the largest possible canvas for productivity. The $1,899 starting price reflects the engineering cost of the 4:3 main display and titanium frame.

**Galaxy Z Flip 8** — You want a full-featured flagship that fits in tight spaces, care about camera quality over screen size, and don't need tablet-style multitasking. At $999, it's the more accessible entry into Samsung's foldable lineup.

The Fold 8 Ultra ($1,800+ for 1TB) is the better option if the 200MP camera or 8-inch display matters to you — it replaces the Fold 7 as Samsung's flagship book-style foldable.
