Gemini replaced Google Assistant in Android Auto in two steps. Google began rolling it out globally on November 20, 2025, in 45 languages, starting with people who had already upgraded to Gemini on their phone, according to [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/11/20/gemini-starts-rolling-out-to-android-auto-globally/). Then on September 4, 2026, Google began removing Assistant from phones and phone-projected Android Auto, per [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/08/04/google-assistant-september-2026-shutdown/), so for most people Gemini is now the only choice. Our [Assistant versus Gemini article](https://frontbuffer.net/tech/google-assistant-vs-gemini-what-changes-when-assistant-shuts/) covers what that change means on the phone.

*Checked October 16, 2026 against Google's help pages and press reports. The Assistant-removal details were re-checked on October 21, 2026 against 9to5Google, How-To Geek and Android Police.*

---

## How to start Gemini in the car

[Google's Android Auto help page](https://support.google.com/androidauto/answer/6348083?hl=en) lists three ways to start:

1. Say "Hey Google."
2. Tap the microphone on the car screen.
3. Press and hold the voice command button on the steering wheel.

Wait for the tone, then speak. TechCrunch adds that a tooltip appears on the car display once Gemini is available for you.

---

## What you can ask it

Google says Gemini understands the same commands as Assistant and also lets you speak naturally. Examples from Google and the launch coverage:

- **Calls and routes.** Google's [Gemini help page](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16735982) suggests "Hey Google, call Mom" or "start my commute playlist," and a harder request such as finding a supercharger near a sushi restaurant that is open now. Google's [calls help page](https://support.google.com/androidauto/answer/6348066) says Gemini can call someone by relationship, such as "call my sister," if that relationship is saved in your instructions for Gemini.
- **Messages.** Google's [messages help page](https://support.google.com/androidauto/answer/6348317) says Gemini can help write a message without you dictating it word for word, and can summarize your messages. TechCrunch adds replies that include your ETA and translating a message before it is sent.
- **Email and music.** TechCrunch describes checking Gmail, such as finding a hotel address in an email and navigating there, plus summaries of unread email and playlists built on YouTube Music, Spotify and similar services.
- **Places along a route.** Asking about businesses on your route, such as a barbecue spot that is open near your destination.

---

## Gemini Live

For a free-flowing conversation, Google's Android Auto page says to say "Hey Google, let's talk" or "Hey Google, let's talk live." TechCrunch reported the phrase as "let's chat," and Google's Gemini page says to tap the microphone and say "Let's talk Live." The sources differ, so try the version Google's own pages give first.

---

## Limits and cautions

- **Google says it can be wrong.** Its Gemini page says Gemini in Android Auto is new and evolving, can present inaccurate information as fact, and should not be relied on for critical or safety-related matters while driving.
- **Anyone in the car can use it,** including children, according to the same page.
- **Languages differ.** Gemini began rolling out in 45 languages. [Google's Android Auto page](https://www.android.com/auto) notes that some languages do not support "Hey Google" voice commands or some Gemini action features.
- **Reliability is unsettled.** [How-To Geek wrote](https://www.howtogeek.com/google-assistant-shuts-down-on-android-in-september/) that Gemini has struggled with simple commands such as direct device controls, and [Android Police reported](https://www.androidpolice.com/the-google-assistant-kill-off-has-arrived/) on September 29 that many users were unhappy about losing Assistant. We did not find an independent test of Gemini in Android Auto.

---

## If it does not show up

Google's Android Auto page says the transition to Gemini is a server-side update that rolls out over time, and that you should have Gemini set as your primary mobile assistant. Google's setup page also says your phone might ask you to update Android Auto before you continue. 9to5Google reports that Google said the Assistant removal that began on September 4 may take a few weeks to reach every device.

A practical order: confirm Gemini is your phone's assistant, update Android Auto and the Google app, reconnect to the car, and give a staged rollout a few days.

## Our reading of the sources

Google's help pages describe what Gemini in Android Auto is meant to do, and the evidence about how well it does it is mostly anecdotal. How-To Geek and Android Police report problems, and we did not find an independent test. Our reading is that the capability list above is Google's intent, not a guarantee, and that Google's own warning against relying on it for safety-related matters while driving is the line to take seriously.

Most "it does not show up" cases look like rollout timing, since Google calls the transition a server-side update and 9to5Google says the Assistant removal can take a few weeks to reach every device. The practical order above is the cheap path: confirm Gemini is your phone's assistant, update the apps, reconnect, and wait a few days before changing anything else. If your language is on the list that lacks "Hey Google" support, the tap and steering-wheel methods are the ones to try. This is our judgment from the sources, not a test.
