A digital game purchase is a license, not ownership. Steam's cart says so directly, and in July 2026 Sony announced two changes that bear on the physical-versus-digital choice: it will stop producing discs for new PlayStation games in January 2028, and it is closing the PS3 and PS Vita stores between August 2026 and July 2027.

*Checked October 20, 2026 against the PlayStation Blog, GameSpot, Quartz, GamingOnLinux and a law-firm summary of California's AB 2426. Dates can change, and this is general information, not legal advice.*

---

## What a digital purchase gives you

[GamingOnLinux reports](https://gamingonlinux.com/2024/10/steam-purchases-now-clearly-state-youre-just-getting-a-license-not-ownership) that Steam's checkout shows "A purchase of a digital product grants a license for the product on Steam," and quotes the Steam Subscriber Agreement: the content and services are licensed, not sold, and the license confers no title or ownership. A law firm's [summary of California's AB 2426](https://mofo.com/resources/insights/250318-a-new-california-digital-content-law-establishes) says the law, effective January 1, 2025, requires sellers to disclose when consumers get a revocable license to digital goods, including games, rather than ownership.

---

## What a disc gives you

A disc is a physical object you can lend, sell or keep, and it does not depend on one account the way a digital library does. That is general reasoning rather than something these sources test.

---

## What changed in 2026 for PlayStation

Sony's July 1, 2026 announcements matter most for PlayStation players:

- **Discs:** [GameSpot reports](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/sony-confirms-playstation-disc-production-will-end-in-2028/) that Sony will end physical disc production starting January 2028, so all new first-party and third-party games will be available digitally only, through the PlayStation Store and at retailers in digital formats. Sony says the change has no impact on games already released or released before then. [Quartz](https://qz.com/playstation-physical-disc-production-ending-2028-070126) quotes the PlayStation Blog's wording that disc production for all new games releasing on PlayStation consoles will be discontinued starting January 2028.
- **Stores:** The [PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/07/01/an-update-on-playstation-store-for-ps3-and-ps-vita/) says the PS3 store closes from August 2026 in Mexico, Honduras and Nicaragua, from late 2026 in additional Latin American and Middle Eastern countries, and in July 2027 for PS3 and PS Vita in all other countries. Sony says players can still download previously purchased content after closing "for the foreseeable future."

---

## Storefront closures are not new

In March 2021 Sony announced closures for the PS3, PSP and Vita stores. [Axios reported](https://www.axios.com/2021/04/19/sony-ps3-vita-stores) on April 19, 2021 that Sony reversed course for PS3 and Vita, with Jim Ryan saying "we made the wrong decision," while the PSP store still closed on July 2. The 2026 announcement revives that plan, with Sony citing the old consoles' inability to support updated payment standards.

---

## Which to choose

- **Physical:** a better fit if you want to lend or resell, and for PlayStation games releasing before January 2028. After that, Sony says new releases will not be on disc.
- **Digital:** a better fit if you want everything on one account and instant access, with the understanding that you hold a license and that storefronts can close.

Many players use both. The 2026 changes mean the physical option is shrinking on PlayStation, so buy discs of games you care about while they are still made.
