The Galaxy Z Fold 8 went on sale on [August 7, 2026](https://news.samsung.com/uk/samsung-officially-launches-galaxy-z-fold8-ultra-z-fold8-z-flip8-watch-ultra2-and-watch9) and ships with Android 17 and One UI 9, per [Samsung's product page](https://www.samsung.com/us/smartphones/galaxy-z-fold8/). These five steps cover the first day. Menu labels below come from Samsung support pages and earlier One UI versions, and they were not checked on a Fold 8, so use the Settings search bar if a label differs.

## 1. Update the phone and sign in

Open Settings, then Software update, then Download and install, and apply anything pending before you set up apps. Sign in with a Google account for Play Store and app restores, and with a Samsung account. Samsung's Fold 8 page says features such as [Now Nudge and My FanCam need a Samsung account login](https://www.samsung.com/us/smartphones/galaxy-z-fold8/).

## 2. Move your data with Smart Switch

On the Fold 8, Samsung describes starting a transfer by [scanning a QR code](https://www.samsung.com/us/smartphones/galaxy-z-fold8/). From an iPhone, it says no Smart Switch app is needed on the iPhone. That wireless method requires the Galaxy to run Android 17 and One UI 9 or later, and the iPhone to run iOS 26.6 or later. Samsung's list of transferable data includes contacts, photos, videos, messages, notes, passwords and passkeys, eSIMs, saved Wi-Fi networks, and apps with their data.

From an Android phone, Samsung's Fold 7 page says to [open Smart Switch Mobile in Settings](https://www.samsung.com/levant/smartphones/galaxy-z-fold7/) on the new phone. The same page recommends a wired connection when you transfer many kinds of data at once. That recommendation is from the Fold 7 page and was not repeated for the Fold 8.

## 3. Set up the fingerprint reader, and learn what this Fold lacks

The fingerprint reader is built into the power button on the right edge, as [Gizmodo's reviewer](https://gizmodo.com/samsung-galaxy-z-fold-8-review-the-most-fun-phone-in-ages-2000792547) and [FoneArena's unboxing](https://www.fonearena.com/blog/?p=488478) both describe. Register it under Settings, then Security and privacy, then Biometrics.

While you are in setup, know what is missing so you do not hunt for it. Samsung's FAQ says the Fold 8 [has no Flex Mode, no S Pen support and no SD card slot](https://www.samsung.com/us/smartphones/galaxy-z-fold8/). It explains that the hinge is built for fully open or fully closed use, not partly open positions.

## 4. Check the cover screen and split view

The cover screen is 5.5 inches at 10:16 and the inner screen is 7.6 inches at 4:3, per [Samsung](https://www.samsung.com/us/smartphones/galaxy-z-fold8/). Samsung says One UI 9 supports two-way and three-way split view on the inner screen, and that Now Nudge can carry a suggestion from the cover screen into a split view when you unfold the phone.

Open the five apps you use most on both screens. The Samsung pages checked for this article do not list which apps are tuned for these two aspect ratios, so any app that crops or stretches video is worth noting before you rely on it.

## 5. Set the refresh rate and battery protection

**Refresh rate.** Search Settings for Motion smoothness. Samsung's support page describes Standard as a fixed 60Hz for the best battery life, and Adaptive as adjusting dynamically up to 120Hz. It also says Power saving mode forces Standard. That page was written for earlier models. Reviews describe the Fold 8's screens as [120Hz LTPO panels](https://www.fonearena.com/blog/?p=488478), so Adaptive is the setting to try first. Samsung's page for the [refresh rate options](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10003396/) has the steps.

**Battery protection.** Open Settings, then Battery, then Battery protection. Samsung's support page describes [Basic as the default, which limits extra charging after 100%, and Maximum, which stops at a limit you pick from 80, 85, 90 or 95%](https://www.samsung.com/uk/support/galaxy-battery/battery-protection-tips/). It says a lower limit can help manage battery life, and describes a sleep-time option that holds the charge near 80% overnight. Samsung's Middle East support page still describes [Maximum as an 85% cap](https://www.samsung.com/ae/support/mobile-devices/what-are-the-specific-differences-of-the-battery-protection-options/), so options differ by region and software version. Check what your Fold shows.

The battery is 4,800mAh. Samsung quotes up to 26 hours of video playback, measured in its own lab with Bluetooth earphones over LTE, so everyday use will differ.

## Our reading of the five steps

Steps 1, 2 and 5 change settings, and steps 3 and 4 are about knowing the hardware. We would run the settings steps in the order given, update and sign in first, because Samsung's wireless transfer from an iPhone needs the Galaxy to run Android 17 and One UI 9 or later, which is what the phone ships with.

On battery protection, treat Samsung's percentages as regional examples, not a promise for your phone. Samsung's UK page describes limits of 80, 85, 90 or 95%, while its Middle East page describes Maximum as an 85% cap. Check what your Fold shows. On refresh rate, Adaptive is the setting to try first, since Samsung describes Standard as a fixed 60Hz aimed at battery life.

The lines about what the Fold 8 lacks are most useful if you are still deciding whether to keep it: no Flex Mode, no S Pen support and no SD card slot, according to Samsung's FAQ. This is our reading of the sources above. The menu labels were not checked on a Fold 8.

## Questions readers ask

**How do I set up a Galaxy Z Fold 8?** Update the phone and sign in to your accounts, move your data with Smart Switch, set up the fingerprint reader and learn what the phone lacks, check the cover screen and split view, then set the refresh rate and battery protection.

**How do I transfer from an iPhone?** Samsung describes scanning a QR code on the Fold 8, with no Smart Switch app needed on the iPhone. It requires the Galaxy to run Android 17 and One UI 9 or later, and the iPhone to run iOS 26.6 or later.

**Does the Fold 8 have an S Pen or an SD card slot?** Samsung's FAQ says it has no S Pen support, no SD card slot and no Flex Mode, because the hinge is built for fully open or fully closed use.

**Where is the fingerprint reader?** In the power button on the right edge, as Gizmodo's reviewer and FoneArena's unboxing both describe.

**How big is the battery?** 4,800mAh. Samsung quotes up to 26 hours of video playback from its own lab test with Bluetooth earphones over LTE, so everyday use will differ.
