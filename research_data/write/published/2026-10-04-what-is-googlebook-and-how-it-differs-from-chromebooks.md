Googlebook is Google's new laptop platform. It runs Android apps from Google Play, builds Gemini into the cursor and the OS, and is designed to work with your Android phone. Google announced it in May 2026, opened preorders on September 21, and US shipping began October 4. Five launch models come from Acer, Dell, ASUS, HP, and Lenovo, priced from $899.

*Details checked October 6, 2026.*

---

## What Googlebook is

Googlebook laptops run Googlebook OS rather than ChromeOS. Google's launch material labels the hardware "Designed for Gemini Intelligence," and each laptop has a glow bar built into it. Coverage of the launch describes the OS as combining Chrome, Google Play apps, and an AI layer, so the browser is still there, but it is no longer the foundation.

---

## The main features

These come from [Google's launch post on Googlebook's built-in intelligence](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/googlebook/googlebook-built-in-intelligence/):

- **Magic Pointer.** Wiggle the cursor and Gemini appears at the spot you are working in, with an understanding of the text, images, and context under it. Google's examples include selecting several images and asking Gemini to visualize them together. Google says it only runs when you invoke it.
- **Rambler.** Voice dictation.
- **Create My Widget.** Describe a widget in plain language and Googlebook builds it. Google's examples are a sports score tracker and a countdown to a family trip.
- **Google Antigravity.** Included with every Googlebook, for writing your own apps and deploying them to the laptop.
- **Gemini Spark.** Works on complex requests in the background, even with the laptop closed.

---

## Android phone integration

Googlebook is built to pair with an Android phone:

- **Cast My Apps** opens apps from your phone on the laptop without installing them there.
- **Quick Access** lets you manage your phone's files from the laptop.
- **First sign-in transfer.** According to launch coverage, your settings, saved passwords, Wi-Fi networks, and messages can move over from your phone, protected by end-to-end encryption.

Check which phones and Android versions the phone features support before buying. We haven't found a full compatibility list.

## How it differs from a Chromebook

| | Chromebook | Googlebook |
|---|---|---|
| OS | ChromeOS | Googlebook OS |
| Foundation | Browser-first | Android apps and Gemini first |
| Android apps | Supported, with a compatibility layer | Core to the platform |
| AI | Varies by model | Magic Pointer, Rambler, Create My Widget on every model |
| Phone features | Phone Hub | Cast My Apps and Quick Access |
| Updates | Varies by model | Up to 10 years, per Google |
| Price | Widely below $899 | From $899 |

## Price, bundle, and availability

Prices run from $899 for the Acer Googlebook 14 to about $1,299 for ASUS, HP, and Lenovo. See [our model-by-model comparison]({% post_url 2026-10-02-googlebook-models-compared-specs-and-features-of-the-five-fl %}) for specs.

Each Googlebook includes 12 months of Google AI Pro with 5TB of storage and Gemini Advanced, 3 months of YouTube Premium, and Adobe Photoshop access. In the US, shipping began October 4. Reported launch markets also include Canada, the UK, Ireland, France, Germany, and Australia from October 5. Not every country is covered at launch, so check the Google Store for your region.

---

## What to watch

Magic Pointer's usefulness depends on how well it reads content in apps you actually use. The phone features also depend on your phone. Both are things hands-on reviews will answer better than the launch material.

---

## Takeaway

Googlebook makes sense if you use Android phone apps daily and want them on a laptop, and you are comfortable with a first-generation platform. If you mostly work in a browser, a Chromebook costs less and does the same job.
