Valve now sells two portable gaming devices: the Steam Deck, a handheld PC, and the Steam Frame, a standalone VR headset. Both run SteamOS and use your Steam library, but they answer different questions. The Deck asks whether you want PC games in your hands. The Frame asks whether you want to wear them.

## Specs at a glance

| | Steam Frame | Steam Deck OLED |
|---|---|---|
| Chip | Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 | 6nm AMD APU, Zen 2 CPU, RDNA 2 GPU |
| RAM | 16GB LPDDR5X | 16GB LPDDR5 |
| Storage | 256GB or 1TB UFS, microSD | 512GB or 1TB NVMe, microSD |
| Display | 2160 x 2160 LCD per eye, 72 to 144Hz | 7.4-inch HDR OLED, 1280 x 800, up to 90Hz |
| OS | SteamOS 3 | SteamOS 3 |
| Wireless | Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 5.4 | Wi-Fi 6E, Bluetooth 5.3 |
| Battery | 21.6Wh | 50Whr |
| Weight | 440g with headstrap | About 640g |
| Launch | September 14, 2026 (reservations) | November 16, 2023 |

Frame figures come from [Valve's Steam Frame page](https://store.steampowered.com/hardware/steamframe), except weight, which comes from [UploadVR's coverage of Valve's announcement](https://www.uploadvr.com/valve-steam-frame-official-announcement-features-details/). Deck figures come from [Valve's Steam Deck tech specs](https://www.steamdeck.com/en/tech). The Deck OLED's November 16, 2023 release date is confirmed by [Yahoo Tech](https://tech.yahoo.com/general/articles/steam-deck-oled-announced-features-180632770.html) and [ETCentric](https://etcentric.org/?p=180449).

## Price

**Steam Frame:** $1,059 for 256GB and $1,299 for 1TB, per Valve. The kit includes the Steam Frame Controllers, a wireless adapter and Half-Life: Alyx. It does not include a power supply, and Valve says any 45W or higher USB-C charger works, including a Steam Deck charger. The Frame is sold through a reservation queue, so availability depends on when your purchase email arrives.

**Steam Deck OLED:** $789 for 512GB and $949 for 1TB. [Tom's Hardware reported the increase](https://www.tomshardware.com/video-games/handheld-gaming/valve-hikes-steam-deck-oled-prices-512gb-is-now-usd789-while-1tb-climbs-to-usd949) in May 2026, up from the launch prices of $549 and $649. Valve's OLED page lists a carrying case with the Deck.

## Performance

The Frame's Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 drives two 2160 x 2160 panels at up to 144Hz, which Valve lists as an experimental setting. Valve's developer documentation says it runs [Windows games through Proton, x86 games through FEX and Android games through Lepton](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/steamhardware/steamframe/compatibility). For PC VR, the headset streams from a PC. Valve's support guide, as reported by Mixed, says [adapter streaming has full support on Windows only for now](https://mixed-news.com/en/valve-guide-steam-frame-adapter-streaming-windows-only/).

The Deck's AMD chip runs PC games on the device itself at 1280 x 800. It is a handheld and not a VR device, so VR content is out of scope for it.

## Portability

The Frame is worn. Its total weight is 440g with the headstrap, according to UploadVR, which puts the core module alone at 185g. Its 21.6Wh battery is smaller than the Deck's 50Whr one. The sources checked do not give a Frame runtime, so battery capacity is the only comparison made here.

The Deck is held. It weighs about 640g, and Valve rates its 50Whr battery at 3 to 12 hours of gameplay depending on the game.

## Ecosystem

Both devices use Steam. Valve rates games for the Frame on a [Great on Frame page](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Three-new-games-become-Steam-Frame-Verified-as-Valve-prepares-for-its-launch.1342836.0.html), the way Deck Verified works for the handheld. Half-Life: Alyx comes with the Frame, and UploadVR reports [a native ARM build that runs on the headset without a PC](https://www.uploadvr.com/steam-frame-price-revealed-reservations-opened-alyx-included/). The Deck runs Windows games through Proton.

## Which one fits

**Choose the Steam Frame** if you want VR with your Steam library and have a Windows PC for streaming demanding SteamVR games, and you accept the $1,059 starting price and the reservation queue.

**Choose the Steam Deck OLED** if you want your Steam library in handheld form and do not need VR. It starts at $789.

The two devices target different uses, so the comparison is mostly a question of which use you want.

Specs and prices checked October 15, 2026. The Frame's weight and software details were re-checked on October 21, 2026 against UploadVR and Valve's developer documentation. Nothing here was tested hands-on.
