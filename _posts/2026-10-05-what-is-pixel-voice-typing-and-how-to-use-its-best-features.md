If the microphone is missing from your Pixel keyboard, or dictation stops working, the fix is usually in Gboard or its language settings. Voice typing is a Gboard feature. Google says Pixel 6 and later phones, including the Fold and Tablet, get advanced voice typing, and a newer voice input called Rambler arrived with the Pixel 11. Google's November 10 posts say it is also available on Pixel 10 models.

*Checked October 20, 2026 against Google's Gboard Help pages and 9to5Google's Pixel 11 coverage. On November 18, 2026 we added Google's November 10 Pixel Drop post and its Android blog post for the Rambler section. Menu names vary by Android and Gboard version.*

---

## How to start voice typing

[Google's Gboard Help](https://support.google.com/gboard/answer/2781851) gives these steps:

1. Install Gboard on your Android phone.
2. Open an app where you can type, such as Gmail or Keep, and tap a text area.
3. Tap the microphone at the top of the keyboard.
4. When you see "Speak now," say what you want written.

The first time, Android asks for microphone access, with choices to allow it while using the app, only this time, or not at all. Tap the microphone again to pause. In the voice toolbar you can say a command or tap one of the suggestions, and **Show voice commands** lists more.

---

## Punctuation and line breaks

Google says you can add punctuation by saying phrases such as "period," "comma," "new line" or "new paragraph." Punctuation may not be available in every language.

---

## Commands for Pixel 6 and later

[Google's advanced voice typing page](https://support.google.com/gboard/answer/11197787) lists these for supported Pixels:

- "Delete last word" deletes the last word.
- "Clear" deletes the last sentence.
- "Delete" followed by a word removes that word.
- "Undo" reverses a correction.
- "Stop" ends voice typing.
- You can insert emoji by voice. While you speak, tap **Info** to see more commands.

If you use several languages, Google says voice typing can detect your spoken language automatically on Pixel 6 and later. You can turn that off under Voice typing, then **Auto-switch language**. Under "Improve Gboard & voice typing," **Personalize for you** controls whether it learns from your corrections.

## Rambler on Pixel 10 and Pixel 11

Google's [November Pixel Drop post](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/pixel/november-2026-pixel-drop/) says Rambler can automatically remove filler words like "um" and format your thoughts into clear, polished text, and that you speak naturally, review and send. Its [Android blog post](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/android/gemini-intelligence-november-features-devices/) says Rambler is available on Pixel 10 models in Gboard, in select countries and languages, and is subject to usage limits. The Pixel 10a is not named in either post, so we cannot say whether it gets Rambler.

[9to5Google reports](https://9to5google.com/2026/08/12/pixel-11-gemini-intelligence/) that Rambler, a Gboard feature launched with the Pixel 11, filters run-on sentences and filler words such as "umm" and "ah" to produce a cleaner transcription. When it is active it takes over the keyboard's suggestion strip with a waveform animation, and Gboard settings include an option to use the previous speech-to-text system instead. For the other Pixel 11 differences, see [our Pixel 10 vs Pixel 11 comparison]({% post_url 2026-10-03-pixel-10-vs-pixel-11-camera-low-light-and-voice-typing-compa %}).

## If voice typing is not working

Google's page lists these causes for advanced voice typing:

- **Just set up a new Pixel.** It can take a few hours for the phone to download the necessary updates. Connect to Wi-Fi so they download automatically.
- **Language mismatch.** Your primary Gboard language and device language must be the same. You may need to add the language to Gboard and drag it to the top of the list.
- **Android System Intelligence.** Update that app on Google Play.
- **Microphone permission.** Gboard's help says to allow recording while using the app, or choose Only this time.
- **Older phone.** Advanced voice typing requires Pixel 6 or newer.

Google's pages we checked do not describe offline language packs, so we cannot say which languages dictate without a connection.

## Our reading of the troubleshooting list

Google's list gives five causes but no order. Our reading is that the cheapest checks come first. Look at the phone model, because Google says advanced voice typing requires Pixel 6 or newer and the commands in this post may not apply on an older Pixel. Then check the microphone permission, which is a one-tap fix. Then the language match between Gboard and the device, then Android System Intelligence. If the phone is brand new, the first answer may simply be to wait a few hours on Wi-Fi.

Of the commands, we would learn Undo first, since it reverses a correction, and Stop second. That is a suggestion for beginners, not something Google's page ranks.

Google's November 10 posts now describe Rambler and say it is on Pixel 10 models. The waveform animation and the setting that switches back to the previous speech-to-text system come only from 9to5Google's Pixel 11 coverage, so treat them as reported. We did not re-check Gboard Help for Rambler on November 18.

## Questions readers ask

**How do I turn on voice typing on a Pixel?** Open an app where you can type, tap a text area, tap the microphone at the top of Gboard and say what you want written. The first time, Android asks for microphone access.

**What voice commands work?** On Pixel 6 and later, Google lists Delete last word, Clear for the last sentence, Delete followed by a word, Undo and Stop, and you can say punctuation such as period, comma, new line and new paragraph.

**Why is the microphone missing or not working?** Google's page lists a language mismatch between Gboard and the device, an outdated Android System Intelligence, microphone permission, a new phone still downloading updates, and a phone older than Pixel 6 for advanced voice typing.

**What is Rambler?** Google describes it as voice typing that removes filler words like "um" and formats your speech into clear text, available on Pixel 10 models. 9to5Google reports that it launched with the Pixel 11 and that a settings option can switch back to the previous speech-to-text system.

**Does voice typing work offline?** Google's pages we checked do not describe offline language packs, so we cannot say which languages work without a connection.
