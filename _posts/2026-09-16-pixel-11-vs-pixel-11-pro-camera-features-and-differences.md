Google's Pixel 11 series launched August 20, 2026. The Pixel 11 starts at $899, the Pixel 11 Pro at $1,099. Both run Tensor G6 and Android 17. The $200 gap shows up most clearly in the camera system.

---

## Camera Specs Side by Side

| | Pixel 11 | Pixel 11 Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Main | 48MP, 1/1.56", f/1.7, Macro Focus | 50MP, larger sensor, f/1.7 |
| Ultrawide | 13MP | 48MP, autofocus, Macro Focus |
| Telephoto | 10.8MP, 5x optical | 48MP, 5x optical |
| Max zoom | 30x Super Zoom | 120x Pro Zoom |
| Front camera | 10.5MP, 95° FoV | 42MP, 103° FoV |
| Video | 4K@60fps | 8K via Video Boost |
| Night Sight Video | No | Yes |
| Pro Controls | No | Yes |

---

## Main Camera

The Pixel 11's 48MP main sensor sits on a 1/1.56-inch chip — a measurable jump from the Pixel 10's 1/2-inch sensor. Google claims 56% better light sensitivity over the previous base model. In daylight, the gap between the Pixel 11 and Pro main cameras is narrow. Tensor G6's computational processing closes most of it.

The Pro's main sensor is physically larger, which gives it a consistent edge in low-light conditions where sensor size matters more than processing.

---

## Ultrawide: The Clearest Difference

The Pixel 11's 13MP ultrawide is carried over from the Pixel 10. It works for landscapes but struggles with close-up subjects due to the fixed-focus lens.

The Pro's 48MP ultrawide adds autofocus, which matters for macro shooting and any situation where the subject is within arm's reach. If you use the ultrawide for anything other than wide landscapes, this is worth the upgrade.

---

## Telephoto and Zoom

Both phones use a 5x optical telephoto. The difference is digital zoom ceiling: 30x on the Pixel 11, 120x on the Pro. At 120x, the image is heavily processed, but for distant subjects where you can't close the distance, the Pro's additional range is useful.

---

## Video

The Pixel 11 records 4K at 60fps. The Pixel 11 Pro adds Video Boost — Google's server-side HDR processing that enables 8K capture. Video Boost requires a Google AI Pro subscription for higher usage tiers and uploads footage to Google's servers before final versions are returned.

Night Sight Video is Pro-only. For low-light video — concerts, dim restaurants, outdoor evenings — this is the single most practical exclusive on the Pro.

---

## Front Camera

42MP vs 10.5MP is a large gap in resolution. The Pro's wider 103-degree field of view also fits more people into group selfies. If front camera quality matters to you, the Pro is the clear choice.

---

## Who Should Buy Which

**Pixel 11** — Daylight shooting, no need for ultrawide autofocus, 4K video is enough, selfie quality isn't a priority. The sensor upgrade over Pixel 10 is real.

**Pixel 11 Pro** — Low-light photography or video, frequent ultrawide use, Night Sight Video, or front camera quality. The 8K Video Boost is a bonus for users already on Google's AI subscription.
