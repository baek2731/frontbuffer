The Galaxy Z Flip 8 runs 16 apps on its cover screen out of the box, and Samsung's Good Lock MultiStar module still covers everything else. The Flip 8, which went on sale August 7, 2026, also lets you start the MultiStar setup from the cover screen itself. Here is what is supported and how to add the rest.

*Checked October 6, 2026. Samsung changes the cover screen app list through updates, so confirm against your phone.*

---

## Apps supported out of the box

Samsung ships the Flip 8 with 16 supported cover screen apps. [Android Authority reports](https://www.androidauthority.com/galaxy-z-flip-8-cover-screen-apps-3688143/) that 12 are Samsung system apps, fully optimized for the cover screen, and they live in a native app drawer made for the Flex Window. These are: Bixby, Samsung Browser, Calculator, Calendar, Camera, Clock, Contacts, Gallery, Samsung Health, Notes, Phone, and Voice Recorder.

The other four are third-party apps. The apps we've seen named include Google Maps, Google Messages, Netflix, and YouTube. Samsung lists some third-party apps under a **Labs** section because they may not work perfectly on the small screen. Outlets count the Labs list differently: 9to5Google reported about 50 apps officially supported through it at launch. Open the Labs section on your phone for the current list.

The Flip 8 also adds a Recents page on the cover screen and better Now Bar support.

---

## What is new on the Flip 8

- **MultiStar setup from the cover screen.** Earlier Flips prompted you to install MultiStar only from the cover screen settings with the phone unfolded. The Flip 8 shows the prompt on the cover screen itself.
- **Built-in aspect ratio button.** Android Authority found that One UI 9 on the Flip 8 includes a button to change an app's aspect ratio on the cover screen, so you don't need MultiStar for it. It works with official apps, Labs apps, and unsupported apps you've enabled through MultiStar.
- **Per-app zoom and per-screen auto-rotate lock.**
- **Possible Flip 7 update.** Android Authority and Digital Trends report Samsung plans to bring the updated cover screen experience to the Flip 7. It isn't clear whether older Flips will get it.

---

## How to add any app with MultiStar

For apps outside the supported list, use MultiStar, a module inside Samsung's Good Lock suite. These steps work on current versions, but menu names can change between releases.

**Step 1: Install Good Lock.** Open the Galaxy Store, search "Good Lock," and install it. If you see the MultiStar prompt on the cover screen, you can start from there instead.

**Step 2: Install MultiStar.** In Good Lock, open the **Life Up** tab, find **MultiStar**, and install it.

**Step 3: Choose your apps.** Open MultiStar, tap **I ♡ Galaxy Foldable**, select **Launcher Widget**, and pick the apps you want on the cover screen.

**Step 4: Add the widget.** Go to **Settings → Cover screen → Widgets**, find **Good Lock Launcher**, and add it.

Then close the phone, wake the cover screen, and swipe to the Launcher widget to open your apps. Samsung has also updated MultiStar for One UI 9, and newer versions may offer a way to launch apps from the cover screen without the Launcher Widget. If your MultiStar shows that option, use it instead of Steps 3 and 4.

Apps built only for full-screen layouts may need scrolling or zooming on the Flex Window. The aspect ratio button and per-app zoom help with that. For quick tasks like checking a map or replying to a message, it works well enough to avoid unfolding.

---

## Other MultiStar features worth knowing

**Multi Focus** keeps apps in split-screen or pop-up view running even when they aren't the active window, which helps if you want a navigation app running next to something else.

**Multi-window Screen Zoom** shrinks apps in multi-window mode to fit more on screen. It matters more on a main display than on the cover screen.

**Samsung DeX enhancements** add higher-resolution output options and raise the simultaneous app limit beyond DeX's default of 20 for users who connect an external display.

---

## Takeaway

Check the native list and the Labs section first, since most daily apps are already covered. Add MultiStar only for the apps that are missing. Look for the built-in aspect ratio button before you go hunting for settings in MultiStar.
