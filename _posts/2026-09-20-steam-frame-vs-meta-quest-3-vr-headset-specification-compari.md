The Steam Frame starts at $1,059 and the Meta Quest 3 costs $599.99, so the first question is whether the extra money buys something you will use. Valve lists the [256GB Steam Frame kit at $1,059 and the 1TB kit at $1,299](https://store.steampowered.com/hardware/steamframe). Each kit includes the headset, Steam Frame Controllers, a wireless adapter and Half-Life: Alyx, but not a power supply. Meta's own store sells the [Quest 3 in one configuration, 512GB, for $599.99](https://www.meta.com/quest/quest-3/buy-now/).

## Specs side by side

| | Steam Frame | Meta Quest 3 |
|---|---|---|
| Chip | Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 | Snapdragon XR2 Gen 2 |
| RAM | 16GB LPDDR5X | 8GB |
| Display | 2160 x 2160 LCD per eye | 2064 x 2208 LCD per eye |
| Refresh rate | 72 to 144Hz (144Hz experimental) | 120Hz |
| Storage | 256GB or 1TB UFS, microSD slot | 512GB |
| Passthrough | Monochrome | Color, 4MP |
| OS | SteamOS 3 | Meta Horizon OS |
| PC streaming | Included Wi-Fi 6E adapter | Meta Quest Link (cable or Air Link) |
| Price (US) | $1,059 / $1,299 | $599.99 |

Frame figures come from [Valve's Steam Frame page](https://store.steampowered.com/hardware/steamframe), except passthrough, which both [UploadVR](https://www.uploadvr.com/valve-steam-frame-official-announcement-features-details/) and [Club386's review](https://www.club386.com/steam-frame-review/) describe as monochrome. Quest 3 figures come from [Meta's developer comparison page](https://developers.meta.com/vr/resources/compare-devices/) and its [store page](https://www.meta.com/quest/quest-3/buy-now/).

## How you buy each one

You cannot simply order a Steam Frame. [Beebom reports](https://beebom.com/valve-opens-steam-frame-reservations/) that Valve opened reservations on September 14, 2026, with sign-ups open until September 17 at 10 AM Pacific, a randomized queue, and purchase emails from September 18 that give you 72 hours to check out. The Quest 3 is on sale directly from Meta.

Valve says any USB-C charger of 45W or higher works with the Frame, and that [a Steam Deck charger works too](https://store.steampowered.com/hardware/steamframe), so budget for one if you do not own it.

## PC streaming

The Frame's headset has Wi-Fi 7 with two radios, and the box includes a Wi-Fi 6E USB adapter that plugs into your PC. UploadVR describes it as [a direct 6GHz connection between the headset and the dongle](https://www.uploadvr.com/steam-frame-price-revealed-reservations-opened-alyx-included/), plus a Multi-Link Streaming mode that uses the adapter and your Wi-Fi at the same time and picks the lowest-latency path per packet. The Quest 3 streams through Meta Quest Link over a cable or Air Link, per Meta.

We did not find an independent latency comparison between the two systems, so how they feel in use is not covered here.

## Software and standalone play

The Frame runs SteamOS 3. Valve's developer documentation says it runs Windows games through [Proton, x86 games through FEX, and Android games through Lepton](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/steamhardware/steamframe/compatibility), so compatibility varies by game. Valve rates titles on a [Great on Frame page](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Three-new-games-become-Steam-Frame-Verified-as-Valve-prepares-for-its-launch.1342836.0.html), using Verified and Playable ratings. Check that your games are listed before you buy for standalone play.

Half-Life: Alyx is included with the Frame. UploadVR reports a [native ARM build that runs without a PC](https://www.uploadvr.com/steam-frame-price-revealed-reservations-opened-alyx-included/). [Gaming on Steam reports](https://gamingonsteam.com/2026/09/14/steam-frame-preorders-are-open-prices-specs-and-half-life-alyx) that you redeem it in the headset's settings and cannot redeem a second copy if your account already owns it.

The Quest 3 runs Meta Horizon OS and its own store. Which store carries the games you play is the question that matters, and it is worth checking title by title.

## Passthrough

The Quest 3 has color passthrough from 4MP cameras plus a depth sensor, according to Meta. UploadVR and Club386 describe the Frame's passthrough as monochrome. If you use mixed reality, that is a clear difference in the Quest 3's favor.

## Which one fits

**Choose the Steam Frame** if your game library is on Steam, you want a dedicated wireless link to a gaming PC, and you accept the higher price and the reservation queue. It has twice the Quest 3's RAM and a higher refresh ceiling.

**Choose the Meta Quest 3** if you want to buy today at $599.99, you use mixed reality, or you mainly play games from Meta's store.

Specs and prices checked October 15, 2026 against Valve and Meta. Passthrough, reservation, Half-Life: Alyx and software details were re-checked on October 21, 2026 against UploadVR, Club386, Beebom, Gaming on Steam and Valve's developer documentation. Nothing here was tested hands-on.
