The Galaxy S26 Ultra has two telephoto lenses and the Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra has one. That is the biggest camera difference between them, and it runs against the idea that the foldable is the one with extra zoom hardware. The S26 Ultra [went on sale March 11, 2026](https://www.androidcentral.com/phones/samsung-galaxy/samsung-galaxy-s26-release-date). The Fold 8 Ultra followed on [August 7](https://news.samsung.com/uk/samsung-officially-launches-galaxy-z-fold8-ultra-z-fold8-z-flip8-watch-ultra2-and-watch9). Both use the [Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy](https://www.three.co.uk/mobile-phones/brands/samsung/compare/samsung-galaxy-fold8-ultra-vs-samsung-galaxy-fold7), according to [Three UK's Fold 8 Ultra listing and Gizmochina's S26 Ultra report](https://www.gizmochina.com/?p=728485).

## Camera specs side by side

| | Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra | Galaxy S26 Ultra |
|---|---|---|
| Main | 200MP, f/1.7, OIS | 200MP, f/1.4 |
| Ultrawide | 50MP, f/1.9 | 50MP, f/1.9 |
| 3x telephoto | 10MP, f/2.4, OIS | 10MP, f/2.4 |
| 5x periscope | None | 50MP, f/2.9 |
| Selfie | 10MP cover + 10MP inner, f/2.2 | 12MP, f/2.2 |
| Zoom range Samsung lists | 3x optical, up to 30x digital | 3x and 5x optical, 10x optical quality, up to 100x |
| Screens | 8.0-inch inner, 6.5-inch cover | 6.9-inch |

Fold 8 Ultra figures come from [Three UK's comparison page](https://www.three.co.uk/mobile-phones/brands/samsung/compare/samsung-galaxy-fold8-ultra-vs-samsung-galaxy-fold7), which reproduces Samsung's spec list, and from [Samsung's Fold 8 page](https://www.samsung.com/us/smartphones/galaxy-z-fold8/). S26 Ultra figures come from [Samsung's S26 Ultra listing](https://www.samsung.com/za/smartphones/galaxy-s/galaxy-s26-ultra-silver-shadow-256gb-sm-s948bzsiafa/), apart from the 100x figure and screen size, which come from [Gizmochina's launch report](https://www.gizmochina.com/?p=728485).

## Main camera: wider lens on the S26 Ultra

Both phones use a 200MP main camera, but the S26 Ultra's lens is f/1.4 against the Fold 8 Ultra's f/1.7. Samsung's S26 Ultra page credits the wider aperture with a [47% brightness improvement on the 200MP camera](https://www.samsung.com/za/smartphones/galaxy-s/galaxy-s26-ultra-silver-shadow-256gb-sm-s948bzsiafa/), measured against the previous Ultra rather than the Fold. The ratio of the two f-numbers squared gives a similar figure, about 1.5 times the light through the lens. That is an aperture-only calculation. It ignores sensor size, which Samsung's pages do not publish for either phone, and image processing.

## Telephoto: the S26 Ultra has more

The Fold 8 Ultra has a 10MP 3x telephoto and nothing longer. Past 3x it relies on digital zoom, which Samsung lists as up to 30x. The S26 Ultra adds a 50MP 5x periscope lens, and Samsung labels [5x as optical zoom and 10x as optical quality zoom](https://www.samsung.com/za/smartphones/galaxy-s/galaxy-s26-ultra-silver-shadow-256gb-sm-s948bzsiafa/) from that lens. If you regularly shoot at distance, this is the spec that separates them.

## Selfies: different tradeoffs

The Fold 8 Ultra has a 10MP selfie camera on each screen, both f/2.2. The S26 Ultra has one 12MP f/2.2 camera. Samsung's Fold 8 page says the Fold 8 line's [inner screen camera has a 100-degree field of view for group selfies](https://www.samsung.com/us/smartphones/galaxy-z-fold8/). That figure is given for the standard Fold 8. Whether the Ultra's inner camera matches it was not confirmed for this article.

## What the Fold 8 Ultra adds

The advantage is the form factor, not the lenses. The Fold 8 Ultra has an [8.0-inch inner screen and a 6.5-inch cover screen](https://www.samsung.com/us/smartphones/galaxy-z-fold8/). HotHardware says Flex Mode, the half-open stance that puts the viewfinder on the top half and controls on the bottom, [carries over to the Fold 8 Ultra](https://hothardware.com/reviews/samsung-galaxy-z-fold8-and-z-fold8-ultra-review-wide-is-the-way?page=3). The standard Galaxy Z Fold 8 does not have it, per Samsung. Nothing in these specs gives the foldable a camera edge over the S26 Ultra.

## Which one fits

**Choose the S26 Ultra** if photo quality is the priority. It has the wider main lens, the extra 5x periscope and a longer optical zoom range.

**Choose the Fold 8 Ultra** if you want a big inner screen and Flex Mode and can live with a 3x optical limit. On paper the camera tradeoff against the S26 Ultra is the zoom range and the f/1.4 versus f/1.7 aperture.

Specs checked October 12, 2026 against Samsung's pages and a carrier comparison page. No hands-on image comparison was done for this article.
