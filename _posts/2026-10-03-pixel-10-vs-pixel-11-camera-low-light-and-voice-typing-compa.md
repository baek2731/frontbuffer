The Pixel 11's biggest camera change is the main sensor. It stays at 48MP but moves to a 1/1.56-inch sensor, and Google says that gives [56% better light sensitivity than the Pixel 10's](https://9to5google.com/2026/08/12/google-pixel-11-camera-whats-new/). Almost everything else on the camera spec sheet is unchanged, so the upgrade question comes down to how often you shoot in the dark.

This is a spec comparison, not a test. No side-by-side night shots were run for this article, so the low-light expectations below come from sensor size and Google's own claims.

*Specs checked October 15, 2026.*

---

## Camera specs side by side

| | Pixel 10 | Pixel 11 |
|---|---|---|
| Main camera | 48MP, smaller sensor (1/2" or 1/1.95", sources differ) | 48MP, 1/1.56", f/1.7, OIS |
| Ultrawide | 13MP, f/2.2 | 13MP, f/2.2 |
| Telephoto | 10.8MP, f/3.1, 5x optical | 10.8MP, f/3.1, 5x optical |
| Selfie camera | 10.5MP | 10.5MP, f/2.2 |
| Chip | Tensor G5 | Tensor G6 |
| Base storage | 128GB | 256GB |

Pixel 11 specs come from [Android Authority's launch spec table](https://www.androidauthority.com/google-pixel-11-series-3693865/). It says the Pixel 11 keeps the Pixel 10's ultrawide and telephoto cameras. Both phones keep the same 13MP ultrawide and 10.8MP 5x telephoto.

The Pixel 10 series launched in August 2025, roughly a year before the Pixel 11. The Pixel 11 was announced on August 12, 2026, with general availability from August 20. At $899 for 256GB, Google says the Pixel 11 is priced in line with the 256GB Pixel 10, per Android Authority.

---

## The main sensor change

Outlets disagree on the size of the Pixel 10's main sensor. Android Authority says it is a 1/2-inch sensor, and [Notebookcheck lists 1/1.95-inch](https://notebookcheck.net/Google-Pixel-11-launches-with-revamped-camera-and-higher-base-storage.1366481.0.html). The ratio of the squares of 1/1.95 and 1/1.56 is about 1.56, which matches Google's 56% figure. That suggests the larger sensor area is where the number comes from, though Google does not say so.

Resolution stayed at 48MP, so each pixel can gather more light. 9to5Google says the Pixel 10's main sensor was a bit lackluster as part of the trade-off for a true telephoto lens, and that the Pixel 11's [Night Sight is 56% improved](https://9to5google.com/2026/08/12/google-pixel-11-camera-whats-new/) thanks to the new sensor. Those are Google's numbers. Whether the gain is obvious in real shots depends on the scene and on Google's processing.

---

## Low light: what to expect

- **Pixel 10:** Night Sight still works, but the smaller sensor gives its processing less light to start from in very dark scenes.
- **Pixel 11:** the larger sensor should help most in genuinely dark scenes such as concerts, unlit rooms and night streets. In ordinary daylight, expect the two phones to look similar.

That is inference from the sensor change, not a measured result. The ultrawide, 5x telephoto and selfie hardware are unchanged, so a camera-driven upgrade is about the main camera only. The faster Instant Night Sight mode that Google announced is [not on the base Pixel 11](https://9to5google.com/2026/08/12/google-pixel-11-camera-whats-new/).

Android Authority also notes that the Pixel 11 series gets an upgraded image signal processor, which enables 4K portrait video for the first time on a Pixel. That applies to every Pixel 11 model except the Fold.

## Beyond the camera

Google says Tensor G6's CPU is up to 20% more efficient, 25% faster for web browsing and 15% faster for app loading, according to Android Authority. These are launch-material figures, not measurements. Google has not confirmed the chip's manufacturing process, and the 2nm figure that circulated is from leaks.

The Pixel 11's battery is 4,985mAh against 4,970mAh on the Pixel 10, a small increase, per the same article.

For dictation, we cover Gboard's voice typing on Pixel in [our voice typing guide]({% post_url 2026-10-05-what-is-pixel-voice-typing-and-how-to-use-its-best-features %}). Android Authority says Pixel 11 phones will be among the first to get a Gboard mode called Rambler, which makes sense of rambling speech. Whether it comes to the Pixel 10 was not confirmed in the sources checked.

---

## Which to buy

**Keep or buy the Pixel 10** if you shoot mostly in daylight or good indoor light, already own one, or find it discounted since the Pixel 11 launched.

**Buy the Pixel 11** if low-light photos are your main reason to upgrade, you want 256GB as the base storage, or you are buying new and the price gap is small.

---

## Takeaway

If your Pixel 10's night photos already satisfy you, the camera alone is not a strong reason to upgrade. If you often shoot in dim light and wish for cleaner results, the sensor is the one change worth paying for. Read a few side-by-side night comparisons before deciding.
