Googlebooks start at $899. Google calls them flagship laptops, while it describes Chromebooks as built for simplicity, speed and efficiency. So the question is what the extra money buys. In short: phone features that continue tasks and open phone apps on the laptop, Gemini tools built into the OS, premium hardware, a bundle of subscriptions, and up to 10 years of updates. If your work is mostly in a browser, a Chromebook may still cover it.

*Checked October 20, 2026 against Google's Googlebook pages (September 21, 2026), Google's FAQ, TechRepublic, Engadget and PCWorld. We did not price current Chromebook models. Nothing here was tested on a Googlebook.*

| | Chromebook | Googlebook |
|---|---|---|
| Starting price | Varies by model | $899 (Acer) to $1,299 (ASUS, HP), per Google |

## What the extra money buys

**Phone features.** Per Google, Cast My Apps streams your phone's apps into a desktop window, Continue On picks up a task from your phone on the laptop, and the Files app opens your phone's files and photos. Google says some features may need the latest software on your phone and laptop. [TechRepublic reports](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-googlebook-models-compared/) Cast My Apps and a phone-file feature need Android 17 or later. Google describes the Chromebook equivalents as Phone Hub and Quick Start.

**Gemini built into the laptop.** [Google describes Magic Pointer](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/googlebook/googlebook-built-in-intelligence/) as a cursor you wiggle to bring Gemini to what you are pointing at. The same post covers Rambler for voice dictation and Create My Widget. We explain them in [our Googlebook explainer]({% post_url 2026-10-04-what-is-googlebook-and-how-it-differs-from-chromebooks %}).

**Hardware.** Google says every Googlebook uses aluminum, magnesium alloy or carbon fiber and has a haptic glass trackpad and a backlit keyboard, with displays up to 2.8K OLED.

**A bundle.** Every Googlebook includes 12 months of Google AI Pro, which brings 5TB of storage and higher Gemini usage limits, 3 months of YouTube Premium, Adobe Photoshop and more, plus a complimentary year of GeForce NOW. [Engadget lists Google AI Pro](https://engadget.com/ai/googles-20-per-month-ai-pro-plan-just-got-a-big-storage-boost-044502621.html) at $20 a month, or about $200 a year, so the AI Pro portion is worth roughly $200 to $240 if you would pay for it anyway. If you would not, do not count it as savings. Check the offer terms for what happens after each period ends.

**Longer support.** Google says Googlebook OS gets regular feature drops and updates for up to 10 years.

## Who should buy which

**Buy a Chromebook if:**
- Your work is mostly in a browser or Google Workspace
- You want the lowest price, or you are buying for a student or a school
- You do not use an Android phone, or do not care about phone-to-laptop features

**Buy a Googlebook if:**
- You use Android apps daily and want them on a large screen
- You move files between your Android phone and laptop often
- You would pay for Google AI Pro anyway, so the bundle offsets the price

**Wait if:**
- You are wary of a first-generation platform. Hands-on reviews of Magic Pointer and the phone features will say more than launch material
- Googlebooks have not launched in your country yet

---

## Takeaway

Decide on the phone. If an Android phone is central to how you work, the $899 Acer is the cheapest way to try the Googlebook idea. If it is not, price a Chromebook that fits your work and compare it with $899 before paying for features you will not use.
