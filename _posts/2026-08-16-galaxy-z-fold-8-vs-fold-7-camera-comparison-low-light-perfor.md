Two phones carry the Fold 8 name, and only one of them is a camera step back from the Fold 7. Samsung says the [Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra is the continuation of the original Z Fold line](https://www.samsung.com/us/smartphones/galaxy-z-fold8/), while the standard Galaxy Z Fold 8 is a shorter, wider phone with a 4:3 inner screen. [Samsung's launch announcement](https://news.samsung.com/uk/samsung-officially-launches-galaxy-z-fold8-ultra-z-fold8-z-flip8-watch-ultra2-and-watch9) put both on sale from August 7, 2026. So "Fold 7 vs Fold 8" is really a three-way question.

## Camera specs side by side

| | Fold 7 | Fold 8 | Fold 8 Ultra |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main | 200MP, f/1.7, OIS | 50MP, f/1.8, OIS | 200MP, f/1.7, OIS |
| Ultrawide | 12MP, f/2.2 | 50MP, f/1.9 | 50MP, f/1.9 |
| Telephoto | 10MP, 3x optical, f/2.4, OIS | None | 10MP, 3x optical, f/2.4, OIS |
| Selfie (cover + inner) | 10MP + 10MP, f/2.2 | 10MP + 10MP, f/2.2 | 10MP + 10MP, f/2.2 |
| Flex Mode | Yes | No | Yes |

Apertures and lens lists come from Samsung's [Fold 8 page](https://www.samsung.com/us/smartphones/galaxy-z-fold8/) and from Three UK's comparison pages, which reproduce Samsung's spec lists ([Ultra vs Fold 7](https://www.three.co.uk/mobile-phones/brands/samsung/compare/samsung-galaxy-fold8-ultra-vs-samsung-galaxy-fold7), [Ultra vs Fold 8](https://www.three.co.uk/mobile-phones/brands/samsung/compare/samsung-galaxy-fold8-ultra-vs-samsung-galaxy-fold8)). Read side by side, the Ultra keeps the Fold 7's main and telephoto specs and changes only the ultrawide.

## Zoom: the biggest loss on the standard Fold 8

The Fold 8 has no telephoto lens. Samsung lists 2x "optical quality" zoom on both the [Fold 8](https://www.samsung.com/us/smartphones/galaxy-z-fold8/) and the [Fold 7](https://www.samsung.com/levant/smartphones/galaxy-z-fold7/), which its footnote attributes to the sensor's Adaptive Pixel design rather than a separate lens. Past 2x, the Fold 8 is cropping and processing: Samsung quotes up to 10x digital zoom for it and up to 30x for the Ultra. The Fold 7's 10MP lens is a real 3x optical zoom, and it covers the range the Fold 8 can only approximate.

HotHardware's reviewer called [$1,899 a lot for a flagship without a telephoto camera](https://hothardware.com/reviews/samsung-galaxy-z-fold8-and-z-fold8-ultra-review-wide-is-the-way?page=3).

## Ultrawide: the clear gain

The ultrawide goes from 12MP at f/2.2 to 50MP at f/1.9. Samsung says the Ultra's 50MP ultrawide has [wider apertures than the Fold 7's, which it says gives brighter low-light photos and video with less noise](https://www.samsung.com/africa_en/smartphones/galaxy-z-fold8-ultra/compare/). The standard Fold 8 lists the same ultrawide, so the same logic applies on paper. By aperture alone, f/1.9 lets in roughly 1.3 times the light of f/2.2. The Fold 7's ultrawide already had autofocus, per [Samsung](https://news.samsung.com/us/facts-figures-behind-samsung-galaxy-z-fold7-ultra-camera/), so autofocus is not what changed.

## Main camera and low light: not settled

On aperture alone the Fold 7's f/1.7 main lens lets in about 12% more light than the Fold 8's f/1.8. Sensor size matters at least as much, and the Samsung pages checked for this article do not publish it, so no sensor figures are given here. Third-party spec databases disagree with each other on the Fold 7's, which is why they are left out.

What each company says:

- Samsung says the Fold 8's 50MP camera [delivers rich details in low light, with real-time noise reduction from ProVisual Engine](https://www.samsung.com/us/smartphones/galaxy-z-fold8/).
- For the Fold 7, Samsung describes a Spatio-Temporal Filter in [Nightography](https://news.samsung.com/us/facts-figures-behind-samsung-galaxy-z-fold7-ultra-camera/) that analyzes pixel data within and across frames to reduce noise.

No independent low-light test of the standard Fold 8 against the Fold 7 turned up in the searches done for this article (October 12, 2026). Whether the Fold 8's main camera is better, worse or equal in dim light cannot be decided from the published specs.

## Flex Mode matters for the camera too

Samsung's [Fold 8 FAQ](https://www.samsung.com/us/smartphones/galaxy-z-fold8/) says the new hinge is built for fully open or fully closed use and the phone does not support Flex Mode. HotHardware reports that [Flex Mode carries over from the Fold 7 to the Fold 8 Ultra but is missing on the Fold 8](https://hothardware.com/reviews/samsung-galaxy-z-fold8-and-z-fold8-ultra-review-wide-is-the-way?page=3), and describes the camera app in Flex Mode as viewfinder on the top half, controls on the bottom. If you shoot with the phone propped half-open, only the Fold 7 and Ultra can do it.

## Which one fits

**Keep or buy the Fold 7** if you use the 3x lens or Flex Mode. Nothing on the standard Fold 8 replaces either.

**Choose the standard Fold 8** if you mostly shoot at wide and normal focal lengths, want the better ultrawide, and care more about the 4:3 screen and lighter body than about zoom.

**Choose the Fold 8 Ultra** if you want the Fold 7's camera system with the improved ultrawide. Its main and telephoto specs are unchanged from the Fold 7.

Specs checked October 12, 2026 against Samsung's product, newsroom and support pages and one carrier comparison page. Nothing here was tested hands-on.
