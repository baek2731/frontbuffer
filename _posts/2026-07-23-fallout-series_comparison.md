On July 17, 2026, Bethesda Game Studios confirmed in a studio letter that remasters of *Fallout 3* and *Fallout: New Vegas* are in development, but it gave no release dates. [GamesRadar quotes the post](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/fallout/fallout-3-and-fallout-new-vegas-are-both-getting-remasters-bethesda-announces/) as saying that, while it was not announcing any dates, "we have been working on remasters for both Fallout 3 and Fallout: New Vegas." The letter also covered *Fallout 5*, a new Obsidian Fallout game and *The Elder Scrolls VI*.

*Checked October 16, 2026 against press reports of Bethesda's letter. Bethesda's own post was not opened for this article.*

---

## What Bethesda confirmed

| Project | Status in the reports | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Fallout 3 remaster | In development, no date | GamesRadar, Thurrott |
| Fallout: New Vegas remaster | In development, no date | GamesRadar, Thurrott |
| Fallout 5 | In pre-production, built on Creation Engine 3, shared with The Elder Scrolls VI | [PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/games/fallout/fallout-5-guide/) quoting Bethesda |
| New Fallout game from Obsidian | Confirmed | [Thurrott](https://www.thurrott.com/?p=339048) |
| Fallout 76 | A major expansion called Raven Rock next year, a prequel to Fallout 3 | Thurrott |

[KitGuru reports](https://www.kitguru.net/gaming/matthew-wilson/bethesda-confirms-obsidians-new-fallout-game-fallout-5-also-entering-pre-production-2/) that none of these projects has a release date, and that Bethesda teased a special Fallout Day event in Washington next year. Washington is the setting of *Fallout 3*, so KitGuru expects an announcement then, which is its own guess, not something Bethesda said.

---

## The context around the announcement

Thurrott says the letter came after the latest round of layoffs hit Microsoft's Xbox division. [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/2026/07/17/bethesda-confirms-fallout-5-in-preproduction-obsidians-fallout-game-fallout-3-and-new) says the *Fallout 3* remaster was previously leaked in court documents in 2023. GamesRadar notes that the remasters had been heavily requested and rumored since *The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion Remastered* in 2025, and that this was the first time Bethesda had said outright that they are happening.

On who is making what: *Fallout 3* was Bethesda's first game in the series, and *New Vegas* was made by Obsidian Entertainment, according to GamesRadar. Reports say Josh Sawyer, the *New Vegas* director, is [directing Obsidian's new game](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/bethesda-has-finally-confirmed-fallout-5-alongside-remasters-of-fallout-3-and-new-vegas), but Gfinity describes this as reported, not confirmed by Bethesda. The sources checked do not say who is developing the remasters.

---

## What is not known

- **Release dates and platforms.** Bethesda gave neither.
- **What the remasters will change.** The sources checked do not describe changes to gameplay, graphics or the engine.
- **Mod support.** The sources checked do not mention it, which matters if you are planning a modded playthrough now.
- **Whether the two remasters release together.** The letter confirms both are in development, not how they will be packaged.

---

## Quick answers

- **Are the remasters real?** Yes. Bethesda confirmed in its July 17 letter that both are in development, per GamesRadar and Thurrott.
- **Is there a release date?** No. The reports checked say none of the projects has one.
- **Is *Fallout 5* close?** Thurrott says it is likely many years away, since it is only in pre-production.
- **Is Obsidian making a new Fallout?** Yes, confirmed. Game Informer adds that this corroborates an earlier report that Obsidian was told to pivot to the project, canceling an *Avowed* sequel.
